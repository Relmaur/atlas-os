# Lost Artifact

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Artifact
- **Type:** Lost Artifact
- **Value:** 2,296,607.5
- **Used for:** Trading, Trading (High Value)
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Lost Artifact** is a type of Artifact.

## Summary

**Lost Artifact** is one of several types of [[Artifact|Artifacts]] and it is a reward for opening [[Large Artifact Crate|Large Artifact Crates]] found in Ancient [[Ruin|Ruins]], completing [[Mission Board|missions]] (for example [[The Overseer's Tour]] repeatable mission), In suspicious packages (weapons),or completing exploration type expeditions with [[frigate|frigates]].
