# Trading Economy

**Trading** is an [[Economy]] type.

## Summary

Trading [[Star system|systems]] are represented by a stopwatch symbol and are seen as green [[star|stars]] when using the [[Economy Scanner|economy filter]] in the [[Galactic Map]].

Trading systems supply [[tradeable|tradeables]] to [[Advanced Materials Economy|Advanced Materials]] systems and buy from [[Scientific Economy|Scientific]] systems.

## Names

Trading systems can have one of the following names:
- Commercial
- Shipping
- Mercantile
- Trading

## Tradeable

The following tradeables, from lowest to highest value, are produced by Trading systems:

- [[Decrypted User Data]]
- [[Star Silk]]
- [[Comet Droplets]]
- [[Ion Sphere]]
- [[Teleport Coordinators]]

The following tradeables, from lowest to highest value, have high demand in Trading systems:

- [[De-Scented Pheromone Bottle]]
- [[Neutron Microscope]]
- [[Instability Injector]]
- [[Organic Piping]]
- [[Neural Duct]]
