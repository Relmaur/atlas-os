# Scientific Economy

**Scientific** is an [[Economy]] type.

## Summary

Scientific [[Star system|systems]] are represented by a beaker symbol and are seen as dark blue [[star|stars]] when using the [[Economy Scanner|economy filter]] in the [[Galactic Map]].

Scientific systems supply [[tradeable|tradeables]] to [[Trading Economy|Trading]] systems and buy from [[Advanced Materials Economy|Advanced Materials]] systems.

## Names

Scientific systems can have one of the following names:
- Experimental
- Research
- Mathematical
- Scientific

## Tradeables

The following tradeables, from lowest to highest value, are produced by Scientific systems:
- [[De-Scented Bottles]]
- [[Neutron Microscope]]
- [[Instability Injector]]
- [[Organic Piping]]
- [[Neural Duct]]

The following tradeables, from lowest to highest value, have high demand in Scientific systems:
- [[Nanotube Crate]]
- [[Self-Repairing Heridium]]
- [[Optical Solvent]]
- [[Five Dimensional Torus]]
- [[Superconducting Fibre]]
