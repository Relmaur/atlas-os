# Economy

**Economy** is a star system factor.

## Summary

Every [[star system]] in No Man's Sky has its own Economy, which is described by three components: the economy Type, the Strength of the economy, and the Buy/Sell Modifiers it offers. These components are not affected by which [[Faction]] controls the system - any economy Type/Strength/Modifiers can be found for any faction.

Details of a system's Economy are available in the [[Galactic Map]] when your ship is in the system. If you have an [[Economy Scanner]], you can see details for any system from the map, without needing to warp to it.

## Economy Type

Every system has a single economy type (the "Producing Economy" column in the table below), which will show in the system details on the Galaxy Map with one of the names shown in the "Economy Names" column. The icon shown in the map will tell you the type, if you don't want to remember all of the names. Economy type is independent of economy Strength, Buy/Sell Modifiers, and Faction.

## Economy Strength

Except for [[Outlaw Systems]], every system has an economy categorized as weak, average, or strong. The [[Galactic Map]] indicates the strength of the economy using one of the descriptors below. Economy strength determines which Marketed [[Tradeable]] products will be available to buy, with strong economies offering all five products at greater quantities (therefore being the most time-efficient and profitable systems to visit) and average or weak economies offering a smaller selection of products at lower quantities. Strong economies are rarer than average and weak economies.

A positive side-effect of strong economies is that higher-class NPC [[Starship|Starships]] are more likely to spawn in them.

## Buy/Sell Modifiers

Every economy has two price modifiers that will be applied to the prices of Trade Items (items from the list above): a positive Sell modifier applied when selling items, and a negative Buy modifier applied when buying items. Modifiers can be seen in the system's details on the Galaxy Map as +/- percentages. These modifiers indicate a price above/below the galactic average, and apply to Trade Items only - they do not apply to other types of items. Note that the modifiers are not absolutely precise: for example, a Sell modifier of "+50.0%" does not mean that Trade Items sold in the system will always be exactly 50% above the galactic average.

In other words:

- Systems with higher Sell modifiers will pay you more when you sell Trade Items
  - Sell modifiers cap at +80%, so systems in the +70-80% range are optimal for selling goods
- Systems with lower Buy modifiers will charge you less when you buy Trade Items
  - Buy modifiers cap at -30%, so systems in the -20-30% range are optimal for buying goods

Additionally, [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]] at planetary [[Trade Outpost|Trade Outposts]] will offer better prices (for both buying and selling) than the terminals at [[Space Station|Space Stations]]. This applies to all items, not just Trade Items. Likewise, the NPCs who land at Trade Outposts will offer better prices than those who land at Space Stations.

## Black Market Economies

The economies of [[Outlaw System|Outlaw Systems]] are a little different from that of normal, regulated systems. The [[Galactic Map]] will show these systems with "Black Market" in place of the economy strength label, and use a skull icon in place of the 1-3 star rating; you can use this fact to identify pirate systems without needing to have a [[Conflict Scanner]] installed.

Outlaw systems have an economy type like all other systems, but the Trade Items that would normally be produced by that economy are not available to buy. Instead, terminals (and the [[Space Station]]'s merchant NPC) will offer a selection of . (You can still sell the economy type's in-demand Trade Items according to the system's Sell modifier bonus, as usual.)

Smuggled Goods can be transported to any regulated system and sold for a high profit, but beware that system authorities regularly scan the cargo holds of passing ships...

## Trade Routes

Each economy type produces a specific set of Trade Items that may be purchased at a discounted rate (according to the economy strength and the system's Buy modifier), and those items will be in demand by a different economy type where they can be sold at a premium rate (according to the other system's Sell modifier). For example, the items bought from a [[Trading Economy]] can be sold for a much higher profit to an [[Advanced Materials Economy]] than to a [[Scientific Economy]] or [[Mining Economy]]. This creates a cyclical demand between the two sets of economy types:

| Producing Economy | Demanding Economy |
| --- | --- |
| Trade Route A |   |
| Trading | Advanced Materials |
| Advanced Materials | Scientific |
| Scientific | Trading |
| Trade Route B |   |
| Mining | Manufacturing |
| Manufacturing | Technology |
| Technology | Power Generation |
| Power Generation | Mining |

Following a "[[trade route]]" is the practice of traveling between (preferably strong) economies, where the supply purchased at one system is sold for high profit in the next, then that system's supply is purchased and sold at the next system, and so on. Use the Buy and Sell Modifiers to determine which systems to include in your route(s).

## Galactic Map Identification

The Economy can be shown in the galactic map view easily by using the economy filter, which requires [[Economy Scanner]] to be installed on the starship. The economy types can be identified by filtered system colour code:
- green - Trading
- purple - Advanced Materials
- dark blue - Scientific
- orange - Mining
- yellow - Manufacturing
- light blue - Technology
- red - Power Generation
