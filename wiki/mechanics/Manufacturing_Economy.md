# Manufacturing Economy

**Manufacturing** is an [[Economy]] type.

## Summary

Manufacturing [[Star system|systems]] are represented by a gear symbol and are seen as yellow [[star|stars]] when using the [[Economy Scanner|economy filter]] in the [[Galactic Map]].

Manufacturing systems supply [[tradeable|tradeables]] to [[Technology Economy|Technology]] systems and buy from [[Mining Economy|Mining]] systems.

## Names

Manufacturing systems can have one of the following names:
- Mass Production
- Manufacturing
- Construction
- Industrial

## Tradeables

The following tradeables, from lowest to highest value, are produced by Manufacturing systems:
- [[Enormous Metal Cog]]
- [[Non-Stick Piston]]
- [[Six-Pronged Mesh Decoupler]]
- [[Holographic Crankshaft]]
- [[High Capacity Vector Compressor]]

The following tradeables, from lowest to highest value, have high demand in Manufacturing systems:
- [[Dirt]]
- [[Unrefined Pyrite Grease]]
- [[Bromide Salt]]
- [[Polychromatic Zirconium]]
- [[Re-latticed Arc Crystal]]
