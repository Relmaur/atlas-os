# Trade commodity

**Trade commodities** are items that can be traded for [[units]] with [[alien|aliens]] or at the [[Galactic Trade Terminal]].

## Summary

Some trade commodities can be found as naturally occurring on planet surfaces such as Albumen Pearls, Vortex Cubes, and Gravitino Balls. Gathering these resources will trigger a detachment of [[Sentinels]].

Trade commodities can also be found in [[Cargo Drop|Cargo Drops]].

Some of these commodities are also used in [[crafting]].

## Commodities for NPC races

There are three trade commodities corresponding to each of the three alien races:<br>

[[Gek]]:
[[GekNip|Geknip]], [[Gek Relic]], [[Gek Charm]].<br>
[[Korvax]]:
[[Fascination Bead]], [[Korvax Convergence Cube]], [[Korvax Casing]].<br>
[[Vy'keen]]:
[[Grahgrah]], [[Vy'keen Dagger]], [[Vy'keen Effigy]].<br>

These are typically found in the green cases or given at certain events such as monoliths/ruins.<br>

## Commodities for crafting

In addition, apart from the Atlas Stone, there are five trade commodities which are used in crafting: Aquasphere, Gravitino Ball, Dimensional Matrix, Albumen Pearl, and Vortex Cubes.

These five can be found and picked up as hidden/restricted resources in certain locations on certain planets. Doing so in most circumstances will alert Sentinels to attack. As trade commodities all of these can also be found to purchase.
