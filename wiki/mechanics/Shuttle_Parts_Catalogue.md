# Shuttle Parts Catalogue

**Shuttle Parts Catalogue** is an index page.

## Summary

This page contains a list of wing types and parts for the [[Starship Catalogue - Shuttle|Shuttle]] version of the [[starship]].

In total, there are three hull types, nine wing shapes, two thrusters, five chins, six fins, 12 attachments, and 15 accessories. Depending on the model, one ship can display multiple parts. The names used are not game based, but are generally accepted by the community.

**Note:** the items in each list are sorted in order by size.

## Hull Types

This provides the general appearance of the starship, known as the silhouette. There are three general types:
- Double Tube
- Tube Body
- Box Body

## Wings

These nine wing types provide the customized appearance of each starship model:

- Voyager
- Grill Wing
- Y-Wing
- Bent Wing
- Drop-Wing
- X-Wing
- Low Wing
- Glider
- V-Wing

## Thrusters

They can have one of the following two thrusters attached:
- Triple Engine
- Single Engine

## Attachments

The 12 attachments provide extra details:

- Straight Turbine
- Tapered Turbine
- Omega Cap
- Retro Booster
- Fatboy
- Magnatreme Adapter
- Afterburner
- Hover Fan
- Magnatreme Dome
- Magnatreme Ring
- Magnatreme Shield
- Wing Turbine

## Chins

The five chins provide light:
- Rounded Chin
- Fork
- Angled Chin
- Cowl Lights
- Filtration Nose

## Fins

Shuttle starships are stabilised by six different fin types:

- Rudder
- Angled Rudder
- Stabilizer
- Delta Wing
- Shield
- Air Foil

## Accessories

These 15 accessories provide a large customization palette:

- Micro Thruster
- Vertical Intake
- Solar Panel
- Keg
- Mr. Fusion
- R2 Unit
- Fuel Port
- Antenna
- Lunch Box
- Cargo Vent
- Cooling Channel
- Exhaust Cooling Channel
- Angled Vent
- Fuel Compressor
- Coolant Ports
