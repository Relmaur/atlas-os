# Multi-tools - Rail Gun (Pathfinder)

One of the distinguishing features of this type of [[multi-tool]] is the inverted T-shape of the barrel, which appears similar to a train rail mounted on a flat base. This type of multi-tool often has at least one cylindrical extension on the barrel. These usually fall in the Rifle category.
