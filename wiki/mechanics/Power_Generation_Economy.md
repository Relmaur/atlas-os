# Power Generation Economy

**Power Generation** is an [[Economy]] type.

## Summary

Power Generation [[Star system|systems]] are represented by a voltage box symbol and are seen as [[System colours|red]] [[star|stars]] when using the [[Economy Scanner|economy filter]] in the [[Galactic Map]].

Power Generation systems supply [[tradeable|tradeables]] to [[Mining Economy|Mining]] systems and buy from [[Technology Economy|Technology]] systems.

## Names

Power Generation systems can have one of the following names:
- High Voltage
- Fuel generation
- Energy Supply
- Power Generation

## Tradeables

The following tradeables, from lowest to highest value, are produced by Power Generation systems:
- [[Spark Canister]]
- [[Industrial-Grade Battery]]
- [[Ohmic Gel]]
- [[Experimental Power Fluid]]
- [[Fusion Core]]

The following tradeables, from lowest to highest value, have high demand in Power Generation systems:
- [[Decommissioned Circuit Board]]
- [[Welding Soap]]
- [[Ion Capacitor]]
- [[Autonomous Positioning Unit]]
- [[Quantum Accelerator]]
