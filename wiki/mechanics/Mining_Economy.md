# Mining Economy

**Mining** is an [[Economy]] type.

## Summary

Mining [[Star system|systems]] are represented by a pickaxe symbol and are seen as orange [[star|stars]] when using the [[Economy Scanner|economy filter]] in the [[Galactic Map]].

Mining systems supply [[tradeable|tradeables]] to [[Manufacturing Economy|Manufacturing]] systems and buy from [[Power Generation Economy|Power Generation]] systems.

## Names

Mining systems can have one of the following names:
- Ore Extraction
- Minerals
- Prospecting
- Mining

## Tradeables

The following tradeables, from lowest to highest value, are produced by Mining systems:
- [[Dirt]]
- [[Unrefined Pyrite Grease]]
- [[Bromide Salt]]
- [[Polychromatic Zirconium]]
- [[Re-latticed Arc Crystal]]

The following tradeables, from lowest to highest value, have high demand in Mining systems:
- [[Spark Canister]]
- [[Industrial-Grade Battery]]
- [[Ohmic Gel]]
- [[Experimental Power Fluid]]
- [[Fusion Core]]

## Additional information

Occasionally, the player will see NPC starships mine and destroy asteroids with a phase beam.
