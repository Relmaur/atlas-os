# Euclid (Pathfinder)

## Quick Facts

- **Galaxy number:** 1
- **Previous:** Pequibanu
- **Next:** Hilbert Dimension
- **Added in:** Pathfinder

**Euclid** is a galaxy.

## Summary

During the Pathfinder era, the **Euclid** [[galaxy]] resided within the "Outer Ridge" (according to [[Gek]] & [[Vy'keen]] [[Plaque|Plaques]]) in the [[No Man's Sky]] universe. All players initially spawned on a randomly chosen [[planet]] within this galaxy. The galaxy's outer edges have a varied Distance To Centre of approximately 207k LY to 289k LY at various points.  The shape of this galaxy is unknown.

## Pathfinder regions

Regions can be categorized based on a suffix which includes Adjunct, Band, Boundary, Cluster, Expanse, Fringe, Instability, Mass, Terminus, and Void.

It is unknown whether the suffix has a specific meaning, or is simply procedurally generated.
